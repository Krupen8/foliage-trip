🍦 Local things to try (vegetarian)

  • Maple creemee: Vermont's maple soft-serve, at almost any farm stand or shop.
  • Cider donuts: everywhere in October. Ask if they're fried in lard.
  • Vermont cheese: check the label for rennet. Cabot is generally microbial (vegetarian); many aged cheeses use animal rennet.
  • Whoopie pies: Maine's official state treat.

North End tips: ask for vegetable stock in risotto and soups (chicken stock is common), and skip arancini, which usually contain meat.
