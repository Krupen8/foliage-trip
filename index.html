💡 Road tips

📶
Download offline maps. Cell service drops in the Green Mountains and on the Kancamagus. Save VT, NH & ME areas in Google Maps before Thursday.
⛽
Gas up in Lincoln. There are no services along the roughly 30-mile Kancamagus.
🎟️
Kanc parking. The trailhead lots (Sabbaday, Rocky Gorge, Lower Falls) are White Mountain National Forest fee sites. Bring cash or a card for the day pass.
🌬️
Friday wind. Gusts around 45 mph: skip Smugglers' Notch if Rt 108 is closed, and don't light the fire pit in the wind.
⏰
Leave Sat by 8:30. It's about 6½ hours of driving plus stops. Leave early to catch the Eastern Prom sunset (~5:50–6:00 PM).
🗺️
Long routes on a phone. Google Maps on phones may cap waypoints. If Saturday's full route gets trimmed, navigate stop by stop.
🍁
Chase low and east. High peaks and the north are done. Valleys, the east side of the Whites, and the coast are your color.
